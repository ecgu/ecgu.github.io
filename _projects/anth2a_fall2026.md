#### Teaching Section

My teaching section are on Wednesdays. Unlike Egan's course, teaching sections are mandatory, and I will take attendance each week. Each week we will also have graded assignments designed to help you understand the course concepts and prepare for the exams, which make up the bulk of your grade. Attending lecture and teaching sections are thus essential for your success in this course.

#### Office Hours

If you would like to speak one-on-one with me, I will have office hours that by default will be held near the end of the teaching section at ALP 1120. If that time doesn't work for you, then we can work something else out. In either case, please email me at least three days ahead of time so I can plan accordingly.

Office hours are a space where you can ask me for any academic or professional advice: not just questions about the course, but also advice on resumes, applying to grad school, finding resources for disability assistance on campus, concerns about student or faculty conduct, and more. Please don't hesitate to reach out!

#### On Exams

The exams for this course will be done digitally on your laptop/tablet in class. You should have LockDown prepared beforehand. If you cannot figure out how to have it ready, email me at least a week before the exam and we will figure it out together.

#### The Textbook

The textbook, Perspectives (Second Edition), is free and open-source online. You do not need to pay anything for it. If you have trouble downloading it, email me and let me know!
